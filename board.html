Governance

How we govern.

The Global Kidney Foundation operates under strict governance standards ensuring accountability to our patients, donors, and the communities we serve.

📋

501(c)(3) Status

Federally registered nonprofit. All donations are tax-deductible. Annual Form 990 filed in compliance with IRS requirements.

⚖️

Board Independence

Board members serve voluntarily. Conflict of interest policies are strictly maintained across all board positions and decisions.

📊

Financial Transparency

100% of patient-designated donations go directly to program delivery. Administrative costs are separately funded through institutional grants.

🌍

Global Reach

Operating across the United States, West Africa (Nigeria, Ghana), and the United Kingdom — reaching diaspora and local communities simultaneously.

Every donation is a life given a fighting chance.

The Global Kidney Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Your donation is tax-deductible and goes directly to patient assistance programs.
